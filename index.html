About Me

I am a PhD candidate at the School of Automation, Southeast University, supervised by Prof. Xianghui Cao. My research focuses on distributed optimization, federated learning, and communication-efficient algorithms for networked systems.

From August 2025 to October 2026, I am a visiting PhD student at the Department of Information Engineering, University of Padua, hosted by Prof. Luca Schenato. My current research includes communication-efficient interior-point methods for model predictive control and adaptive step-size methods for distributed optimization.

Research Interests

  • Communication-efficient algorithm design in distributed optimization
  • Privacy-preserving federated learning
  • Distributed optimization over networked systems
  • Model predictive control and real-time optimization
  • Applications of distributed algorithms in power systems
